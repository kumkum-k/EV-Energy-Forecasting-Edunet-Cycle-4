# DRIVECAST: EV-Energy-Forecasting-Edunet-Cycle-4

## 🔍 AI-Powered EV Energy Forecasting System
A machine learning–driven solution that predicts EV energy consumption based on factors like **speed, distance, vehicle weight, weather conditions, road type, and driving style**.

---

## 💡 Key Features
- **Comparison of 5 ML Models** (Elastic Net, Random Forest, XGBoost, SVR, and KNN) to select the **best-performing model** for accurate predictions 
- **Accurate Energy Forecasting** using optimized machine learning models  
- **What-If Analysis** to simulate different real-world driving scenarios   
- **Insights for Sustainable Driving**, helping reduce energy waste and promote green mobility  

---

## 👥 Beneficiaries
- **EV Owners:** Better trip planning, reduced range anxiety  
- **Fleet Operators:** Optimized routes and significantly lower operational energy costs  
- **Manufacturers & Researchers:** Deeper insights into battery performance for designing more efficient EVs  
- **Charging Stations & Smart City Planners:** Improved forecasting of charging demand, reducing grid stress  
- **Environment & Society:** Lower energy consumption, enhanced EV efficiency, and reduced carbon footprint  

---

## 🌱 Green Skills Contribution
- Promotes **energy-efficient driving**  
- Enables **data-driven sustainability decisions**  
- Supports **greener transportation systems** using AI technology
